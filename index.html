Bifrost

A project developed to present Bifrost, an intelligent communication hub designed to connect and manage devices seamlessly and efficiently.

Sensors that speak different wireless "languages" (ESP-NOW, LoRa, MQTT) send their messages to gateways, and a Raspberry Pi decides where each message goes, translating between protocols.
